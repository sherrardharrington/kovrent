About Kovrent

Built by both sides of the table.

Kovrent is a holding company that acquires SEC-registered independent RIAs and holds them permanently. No fund clock, no exit, no name coming off the door unless you decide to change it.

What We Believe

The founder decides.

You built it. You should choose the buyer, the timing, and how long you stay.

Entrepreneurial to the core.

We built this firm from zero, the way you built yours. We think like owners because we are owners, and we back ideas that don’t fit the template.

Innovation that earns its keep.

We adopt what makes your firm faster and sharper, modern AI included, and we skip what’s just a demo.

Trustworthy because we know the work.

We know how RIA firms operate: the billing runs, the exams, the client calls, the Monday mornings. Trust here is built on fluency, not promises.

Compliant and diligent, by design.

Everything new we install lives inside a compliance and security framework built for SEC scrutiny, never around it. Diligence is how the innovation stays safe.

We take firms further.

The goal isn’t to preserve your firm in amber. It’s to help it reach a level it couldn’t reach alone, without losing what made it yours.

If this sounds like the way you already run your firm, you’ll feel at home here.

Founding Team

The people behind the promise.

Kovrent was founded by four partners who form the initial management team. Two have spent their careers inside an advisory firm, sitting with clients and answering the same succession question they now ask founders. Two have spent theirs buying companies and keeping them. Every partner has their own capital in every deal.

Where We’re Based

Headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky, with partners in Washington, DC, New York, and Miami.

But most weeks you’ll find us where our firms are: on site during a transition, across the table from a founder, or on the phone with an advisor who has a question that can’t wait.

Careers

Work measured in decades, not deal cycles.

Operations, compliance, technology, and transition people run the firms we hold every day, long after the ink dries. As the firms grow, so does the bench. Open roles are listed at kovrent.com.
