How a Deal Works

How a deal works, first call to close.

An RIA acquisition with Kovrent runs from a fifteen minute first call to a close in about four months. You get a real valuation range within two weeks of sharing three years of financials, diligence follows a fixed request list, and nothing is announced until you decide who hears it first.

First call, fifteen minutes. A real range inside two weeks of seeing three years of financials. Diligence runs sixty to ninety days, quietly. Then documents, close, and Monday. We've moved faster when a founder wanted speed. We've never asked one to.

The Sequence
01

First call

Fifteen minutes. We ask about the firm and the timeline, you ask whatever you want, and nothing gets sent afterward unless you ask for it.

02

The math

About two weeks. You send financials under NDA. We come back with a range and show you how we got there.

03

The offer

A written number with the structure spelled out. Cash, equity, and terms, on one page.

04

Diligence

60 to 90 days. Financials, compliance history, client base, team contracts, technology. Your team barely notices it is happening.

05

Close

Cash at closing. Equity issued the same day.

06

Year one.

The first thirty days, almost nothing changes on purpose. Your clients get a letter you approved, signed by you. Your team meets the two Kovrent people they'll actually work with: one for operations, one for technology. Payroll, titles, and compensation stay exactly where they were. Days thirty to ninety, the back office starts getting better. We move reporting, billing, and compliance workflows onto our infrastructure, one system at a time, tested before switched. Your advisors spend those weeks noticing paperwork disappearing, not learning new software under deadline. The rest of year one runs on the plan we wrote together before close: your calendar, your pace. Some founders work five days a week all year. Some are down to Tuesdays by summer. Both are the plan working. Every quarter, you and a partner sit down and look at the same numbers we look at. No surprises is the deliverable.

What We'll Ask You For

Three years of financials.

Clean or messy, send what you have. We have read worse.

Your current ADV.

It is public anyway. Sending it just saves a week.

A client roster under NDA.

Anonymized is fine to start. We care about shape, not names.

About six hours of your time.

Spread across three months. That is the whole ask.

Questions Founders Actually Ask

My team

What if my team doesn't want to stay? Then we have a problem worth knowing about before we sign, not after. We talk to your senior people during diligence, with your blessing and on your timeline.

Changing my mind

Until the documents are signed, you can walk away and it costs you nothing. We have walked away from deals we wanted. So can you.

My clients

Do we talk to your clients? Not during diligence. Not without you in the room. Ever.

How long I stay

Most founders stay two to three years. We will tell you what we need from you before you tell us what you want.
