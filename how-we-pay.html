How We Pay

The money, on one page.

Four parts: cash, rollover equity, an earnout you can actually control, and sometimes a note. What each one is for, in plain language.

The Structure
01

Cash at close

The majority of the deal, wired the day we sign.

02

Kovrent equity

Part of the price is paid in Kovrent equity. You stop being an RIA owner and become a partner in the company that owns your firm and every firm we buy after it. That equity carries information rights and a say on any sale or rebrand of your firm.

03

Earnout

Tied to client retention, because that is the thing you actually control. Not to revenue targets you cannot influence after you step back.

04

Seller note

Used when it makes the structure work for both sides, not to shrink the cash.
