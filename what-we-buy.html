The Buy-Box

A narrow buy-box, on purpose.

Kovrent acquires SEC-registered investment advisers with $100M to $1B in assets under management, fee-based recurring revenue, and a founder approaching a transition without a successor. We pass on broker-dealer books, turnarounds, and anything we would have to resell to make work.

SEC-registered.

Federally covered investment advisers with a clean regulatory history. State-registered firms and broker-dealer books are outside the box.

$100M to $1B in AUM.

The full range we will look at. Below $100M the economics of a permanent hold don't work. Above $1B the platforms are already calling.

Sweet spot: $200M to $600M.

Large enough that succession is already priced into the firm, small enough that the founder still picks up the phone.

Owner 55 or older, no named successor.

The firm runs on relationships the founder built, and nobody inside can afford to buy them out. That is the problem we exist for.

Off-market, no banker-run auction.

We would rather be the first call than the fifth bid. Four bidders and a deadline is not where we do our best work.

Fee-based recurring revenue.

Advisory fees on standing relationships, not transaction income. We buy firms, not books.

What we pass on: turnarounds, firms built on transaction revenue, and anything we would have to resell to make work. We would rather tell you no today than waste a quarter of your time.
